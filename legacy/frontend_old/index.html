Peer-Reviewed Foundation

Machine Learning & Ethical Design

Built strictly following research parameters from Paper 1 & Paper 2.

🔬 Research Foundation (Paper 1 & Paper 2)

  • WHO 3-Dimension Framework: Emotional Exhaustion, Cynicism/Detachment, Self-Efficacy.
  • 3 Burnout Archetypes: Frenetic (overworkers), Under-Challenged (boredom), Worn-Out (helplessness).
  • Ethical Risk Assessment: Treated as risk signal (like server load/crash prediction), NOT medical diagnosis.

⚡ Machine Learning Pipeline

  • Dataset: 6,000 synthetic rows generated from statistical paper distributions.
  • Models Evaluated: Random Forest vs Gradient Boosting vs Logistic Regression.
  • Explainability (XAI): Integrated SHAP (TreeExplainer) to output per-feature marginal contributions.
